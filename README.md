# 🍫 Global Chocolate Sales & Performance Analytics
### Executive Business Intelligence Dashboard Developed in Microsoft Power BI

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive executive sales performance dashboard tracking global shipment volume, revenue concentration, and regional distribution efficiency for a confectionery enterprise across 2023–2024.

The dashboard integrates transaction-level details (4,998 records) to evaluate product-level revenue contribution, regional penetration across 6 core international markets, and individual sales representative throughput.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack & Analytical Methods
* **BI Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Source:** Multi-market Confectionery Sales Records (Excel/CSV)
* **Dataset Scope:** 4,998 Line items | $27.99M Cumulative Revenue | 1.98M Boxes Distributed
* **Core Functionalities:** Interactive Slicers, Hierarchical Time Trend Drill-Downs, Horizontal Pareto-style Ranking, Conditional Formatting (Data Bars).

---

## 📊 Core Business Metrics & KPIs
* **Total Gross Revenue:** $27.99M
* **Total Units Shipped:** 1,988,952 Boxes
* **Active Geographical Markets:** 6 Countries (UK, USA, India, Canada, New Zealand, Australia)
* **Product Catalog:** 22 Unique Chocolate SKU Offerings
* **Sales Force Size:** 28 Dedicated Regional Sales Representatives

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights & Analytical Findings

### 1. Geographical Concentration
* **Leading Markets:** The **UK ($7.65M | 27.36%)** and **USA ($6.27M | 22.43%)** generate nearly **50% of cumulative global revenue**.
* **Emerging Markets:** India represents the 3rd largest volume hub at **$4.58M (16.37%)**, outpacing Canada, New Zealand, and Australia (~11% each).

### 2. Product Revenue Drivers
* **Top Contributors:** **Orange Choco ($1.86M)**, **Drinking Coco ($1.85M)**, and **99% Dark & Pure ($1.84M)** represent the top revenue engines.
* **Catalog Tail:** Low-tier SKUs like **Milk Bars ($452K)** and **Almond Choco ($475K)** generate less than a quarter of the leading products, suggesting inventory rationalization or bundling opportunities.

### 3. Sales Force Throughput
* **Top Performers:** Representative **Brien Boise** leads turnover with **$2.17M**, followed closely by **Madelene Upcott ($2.02M)**.
* **Performance Spread:** Wide disparity observed between top performers (> $2M) and bottom quartile representatives (< $700K), highlighting an area for quota alignment.

---

## 🖥️ Dashboard Features
* **Executive Metric Cards:** Real-time visibility into overall turnover ($) and total carton throughput.
* **Temporal Revenue Trend:** Multi-year line chart displaying seasonal demand cycles, quarter-over-quarter peaks, and mid-year spikes.
* **Regional Contribution Donut:** Clear percentage attribution by target country with embedded total value summary.
* **Product Ranking Bar Chart:** Horizontal bar visual displaying descending revenue order across all 22 product lines.
* **Sales Rep Matrix:** Tabular leaderboard with embedded data bars for instant visual comparison of rep performance.
